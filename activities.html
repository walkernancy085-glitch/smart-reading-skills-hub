Lesson 1 • Reading Comprehension

🕵️ Story Detective

Read the passage carefully. Then use the clues from the story to answer the questions.

The Community Reading Club

Every Saturday morning, Ama and her classmates meet at the community centre in their town to read together. The reading club was started by their teacher, Mr. Mensah, because he wanted the children to develop a love for books and improve their reading skills.

The community centre is close to the school and has a small reading area with tables, chairs and shelves. Although there are not many books, the learners share the available books and take turns reading.

One Saturday, the group read a story about a farmer in the Eastern Region who planted cocoa, plantain and vegetables. After reading the story, the learners discussed the main idea and identified new words.

At the end of the lesson, Mr. Mensah asked the learners to explain what they had learnt. The children agreed that reading together made learning more enjoyable.

Question 1

Why did Mr. Mensah start the reading club?

Question 2

Where did the learners meet?
